# Third-party components

The studio uses third-party tools under their respective licenses. Their terms are not replaced by this repository's license.

- [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes): Apache-2.0; HTML video rendering and browser preview.
- [Kokoro ONNX](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx): see its upstream license; local speech runtime.
- [Kokoro model](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M): Apache-2.0 model, distributed separately from this repository.
- [GSAP](https://gsap.com/community/standard-license/): GSAP's own license; animation runtime, installed through npm.
- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html) and FFprobe: LGPL/GPL terms depend on the build. Portable executables come from imageio-ffmpeg and @ffprobe-installer/ffprobe. They are not Apache-2.0 tools and are not committed here.
- [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/Home/chromium-projects/): browser and third-party component licenses; downloaded separately by HyperFrames.

User-supplied screenshots, recordings, fonts, music, and logos retain their own rights and are excluded from Git by default. The studio does not grant rights to those assets.
